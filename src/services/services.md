### Apps

No two apps are the same.

Some projects need to move fast and validate an idea before committing to anything heavyweight. For these, a shared codebase across iOS and Android gets you in front of users sooner.

Other projects are worth building natively from day one, particularly if performance, platform-specific feel, platform-specific features matter more than time to market. I'll help you figure out which situation you're actually in.

### Design

Not every project starts with a clear picture of the end result, and that's OK!

If you know there's a problem or product you want but aren't sure what the solution should look like, I can talk your language to determine what needs to be built. Knowing what you need is as important as building it.

As well as system design, I can also quickly build mockups so you can visualise what the end product will look like now, not months down the line.
