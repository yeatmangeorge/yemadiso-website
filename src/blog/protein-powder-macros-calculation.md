# Calculating Macros in Protein Powder

## Calculating Manually

To calculate protein powder macros manually, we need to know the nutritional values per serving and scale them to the amount of powder we want to use. This can be found on the back of the package.

For example, if 30g of your protein powder contains 24g of protein, 2g of carbohydrates and 1.5g of fat, then 40g contains 32g of protein, around 2.7g of carbohydrates and 2g of fat. Simply multiply each nutritional value by the amount of powder in grams, divided by 30.

This works, but calculating every macro for different serving sizes and protein powder flavours can become tedious.

## MacroShake

Luckily, there is a tool that can do this all for us! It's an app called MacroShake. They've done the hard work for us, so we can simply select our protein powder, enter how much protein we want and it will calculate how much powder we need.
