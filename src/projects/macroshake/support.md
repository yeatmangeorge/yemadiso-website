# MacroShake
## Support

Having issues with the app? We're happy to help!

Please send an email to **[support@macroshake.yemadiso.com](mailto:support@macroshake.yemadiso.com)**

We aim to get back to you within two working days. Replies to support emails may be sent from a different email address. Therefore, if you haven't heard back from us, please check your emails for messages from other addresses.
